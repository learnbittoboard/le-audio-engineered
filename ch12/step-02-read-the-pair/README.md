# Chapter 12, Step 02: Read the pair

## Purpose

The two guided readings of Lesson 12, sections 12.5 and 12.6, as worksheets: a unicast bring up and a broadcast sync, each reduced to landmarks to find, facts to record, and questions to answer. Completable read along from the lesson text; completable on the bench from your own capture (a desk expectation: no capture is supplied, and none in the lesson is a transcript).

## Commands

No commands. Work the worksheets:

**Reading one: the unicast bring up.** For a full Act 1 start (advertising, connection, discovery, ASE sequence, CIS establishment, thirty seconds of streaming), record:
- The four landmarks of Lesson 12, section 12.5, with timestamps.
- The ISO interval the stream's rhythm settles into.
- The first retransmission you can find, and its tier (Lesson 12, section 12.4).

**Reading two: the broadcast sync.** For an Act 2 start (extended advertising, periodic train, silent sink sync, BIS streams), record:
- The BIGInfo facts you read: stream count and ISO interval.
- The moment PA sync would have locked, judged from the train's evidence.
- One sentence on what the sink's silence in the capture proves, and one on what it does not.

**Closing question.** Pick one failure shape from Chapter 7 and write the tier and landmark where this witness would see it first.

**Evidence filing.** Save captures as `date-act-event.pcapng` and record with each: the followed device, the sniffer's position, the profile used, and both boards' firmware builds.

## Expected result

A completed worksheet for both readings (every landmark timestamped, every BIGInfo fact recorded), the closing question answered with a tier and a landmark, and captures filed with their four context facts. On the bench, the same worksheet is graded against your own capture file.
